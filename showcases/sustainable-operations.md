# Sustainable Infrastructure Operations

> Oracle commercial planning · Private analysis · Public overview

## Purpose

Build infrastructure whose operating model can support its product ambition. Oracle's private revenue-operations repository connects product usage, provider consumption and planning decisions.

## Engineering and product focus

- Define consistent metrics before comparing adoption or cost.
- Use privacy-minimized aggregate reporting rather than publishing raw user records.
- Separate usage measurement from pricing and packaging hypotheses.
- Make service economics part of capacity and product decisions.

## Current state

The repository documents metric definitions, aggregate query templates, planning models and readiness criteria. This overview was reviewed against repository documentation on October 10, 2026. It does not validate live telemetry, paid demand, revenue or commercial availability.

## Direction

Use measured adoption, retention and operating costs to inform sustainable product decisions as the ecosystem develops. Public evidence can be added when approved aggregate figures have clear observation dates and context.

Pricing hypotheses, forecasts, provider economics and internal procedures remain private.
